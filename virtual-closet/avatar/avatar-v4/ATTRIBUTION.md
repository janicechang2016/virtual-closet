# Avatar v4 third-party assets

The avatar-v4 foundation uses these MakeHuman Community assets:

- `elvs_lady_hippy_hair` by Elvaerwyn — CC BY.
- `elvs_katherine_hair` by Elvaerwyn — CC BY; only fitted fringe geometry is retained.
- `punkduck_high_neck_crop_top` by punkduck — CC BY.
- MakeHuman system body, skin, eyes, and sportsuit assets — CC0.

The assets were fitted, recolored, and assembled in Blender through MPFB.
