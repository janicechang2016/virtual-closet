Ranked by learned preference from the looks you publish — not by colour theory, which measured no better than chance against your own judgement. Telling a card not this asks which piece was wrong: an unattributed rejection can't be assigned blame and would penalise the garments that were fine. Nothing here spends anything; suggestions are your existing cutouts, not renders.

Every judgement is revisable — undo or change on the card, or decisions in the header to revisit anything you have ruled on. Changing your mind appends rather than rewrites: the log keeps what you first thought, and only your current verdict is fed to the model. An undone outfit becomes eligible to be suggested again.
